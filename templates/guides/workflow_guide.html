Inventory Pro Workflow Handbook

Use this guide to move a requisition from request to fulfilment using the warm UI cues introduced with the common design language.

Flow Overview

Each stage below corresponds to a hero banner or toast inside the application.

flowchart LR A[Department raises Indent] --> B[Indent Review & Approval] B --> C{Need PO?} C -- Yes --> D[Consolidate Approved Indents] D --> E[Generate Purchase Orders] C -- No --> F[Issue From Stock] E --> G[Receive Goods (GRN)] G --> H[Fulfil Indent] H --> I[Update Stock Movements] F --> I

Stage Details

  1. Raise Indent — Use New Indent to submit the request. A toast confirms creation and the detail hero explains the next step.
  2. Review & Approval — Approvers change status from Submitted to Approved. The list hero surfaces how many indents are ready for consolidation.
  3. Consolidate Approved Indents — Preview the grouped lines. Submitting creates purchase orders and fires a success toast; an info toast explains when nothing can be consolidated.
  4. Generate Purchase Orders — Quick create or edit orders. The list hero highlights orders awaiting receipts and the detail hero shows supplier/dates.
  5. Receive Goods (GRN) — Record deliveries from the PO detail (Receive Goods) or GRN list. Toasts confirm receipt and the GRN hero records supplier and PO details.
  6. Fulfil Indent — Issue goods from stock. The banner guides you to complete or cancel the indent and a toast confirms fulfilment.
  7. Update Stock Movements — Record quick adjustments, bulk uploads, or wastage. Each modal submits with toast feedback and the hero shows overall metrics.

Verification Checklist

  • Indent creation emits a toast and displays the “Next step” banner.
  • Consolidation creates purchase orders and shows a success toast.
  • Purchase order heroes display supplier/date chips and list banners mention pending receipts.
  • GRN creation redirects to the GRN hero with receipt metadata and toast.
  • Indent fulfilment updates status and emits a toast.
  • Stock movement modals (receive, adjust, waste, quick move, bulk import) trigger toast feedback.

When each box above is checked, the application logic matches the design language applied across the UI.
