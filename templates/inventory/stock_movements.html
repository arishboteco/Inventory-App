Which form should I use?

Receive Stock

Goods have arrived from a supplier. Links the receipt to a Purchase Order and updates stock upward.

Adjust Stock

Correct a stock count discrepancy found during a physical count or cycle count. Use this when the stock ledger is wrong — not when items are spoiled.

Record Wastage

Items have been spoiled, expired, or discarded. Choose this when stock is genuinely lost, so wastage metrics stay accurate.

Bulk Upload

Import many movements at once from a CSV file. Useful for end-of-month reconciliations or migrating from another system.
