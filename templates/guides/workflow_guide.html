Inventory Pro Workflow Handbook

Follow these 7 steps to raise a stock request, get it approved, create a purchase order, receive the goods, and issue them to your team.

Stage Details

  1. Requester

    1. Raise Indent — New Indent

    Fill in the item, quantity, and required date. Click Submit — a green confirmation message will appear at the top of the screen and the indent status changes to "Submitted".

  2. Approver

    2. Review & Approve — Indent Requests

    Open the indent and change its status from "Submitted" to "Approved". A blue banner shows how many indents are waiting for consolidation.

  3. Procurement

    3. Consolidate Approved Indents — Consolidation Planner

    Preview all approved lines grouped by supplier. Click Submit to automatically create draft Purchase Orders — one per supplier.

  4. Procurement

    4. Generate Purchase Orders — Purchase Orders

    Review each draft PO, confirm the supplier, quantities, and delivery date, then mark it as Approved to send to the supplier.

  5. Warehouse

    5. Receive Goods (GRN) — Goods Received Notes

    When the delivery arrives, open the PO and click "Receive Goods", or create a new GRN from the GRN list. Stock levels update automatically.

  6. Warehouse

    6. Fulfil Indent

    Return to the original Indent and click "Fulfil" to issue the goods from stock to the requesting team. The indent status changes to "Completed".

  7. Any Role

    7. Update Stock Movements — Stock Movements

    Use Receive Stock, Adjust Stock, or Record Wastage for any ad-hoc stock changes outside the PO workflow.
